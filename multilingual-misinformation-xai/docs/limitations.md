# Limitations and responsible use

- The current runnable model is trained on English FEVER claims only. It is not an African-language model and does not establish truth.
- AfrIFact files require accepting the dataset's Hugging Face access conditions. Until authorized data are available, its training, language evaluation, and evidence experiments remain pending.
- X-Fact does not list the four target languages in its train or zero-shot inventory and its task/label definitions differ.
- A claim-only model may learn dataset artifacts, entity/topic associations, writing style, or annotation shortcuts. Its probability is not a fact-check.
- FEVER uses claims generated by modifying Wikipedia sentences, which differs from naturally occurring misinformation and current claims.
- Evidence ablation requires verified evidence provenance and matched splits. No claim-plus-evidence performance is reported from the current claim-only pipeline.
- Linear coefficients and local contributions explain the fitted linear score, not causal reasoning or factual correctness.
- Cross-lingual performance, cultural validity, fairness, calibration, and robustness have not yet been measured for the target languages.
- Dataset-card terms, source-material rights, and attribution requirements remain applicable. Raw data and gated samples are not redistributed here.

Do not use this research prototype to moderate, label, or adjudicate real-world claims without independently retrieved and reviewed evidence.
